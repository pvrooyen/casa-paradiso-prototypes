Casa Paradiso prototypes

Four looks for the sale site. Open each on your phone and tap everything.

  1. P1 Paper mapHand-drawn watercolour plot. Lift the roofs, walk the dog to the beach, tap Pedro.
  2. P2 Low-poly 3D toy worldA 3D island you explore by running the dog around. Needs a phone that can do 3D.
  3. P3 Pixel-art retro gameA 16-bit game of the plot with RPG text boxes and real photos.
  4. P4 Photo-first (baseline)A scrolling photo page with a small animated map. The safe fallback, not the playable one.
Prototypes only. Prices and facts are real; the dog's name and Pedro's likeness are placeholders.
