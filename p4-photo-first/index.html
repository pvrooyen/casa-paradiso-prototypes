The pool

A shared splash pool between the big house and the cottages, borehole-fed, with loungers under the palms.

5 minutes to the beach

Casa Paradiso is not on the sand. It sits up the slope behind the dune, which is where the view and the breeze come from. Tofinho beach is about a 5-minute walk down the path.
